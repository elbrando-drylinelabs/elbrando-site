el Brando Harvest Daiquiri

A Thanksgiving twist on the daiquiri. It starts with Puerto Rican silver rum and fresh lime, picks up rosemary-cranberry syrup, a little Cointreau and Angostura, and gets topped with spicy ginger beer for the punch bowl.

  • 12–14 drinks
  • 1 punch bowl
  • Syrup & base made ahead

Ingredients

The punch

  • 1 bottleBacardi silver rum (750 ml, about 25 oz)
  • 12 ozFresh lime juice (14–16 limes)
  • 7 ozRosemary-cranberry syrup (below)
  • 2 ozCointreau
  • ½ ozAngostura bitters
  • 24–32 ozSpicy ginger beer, chilled

Rosemary-cranberry syrup

Makes about 1½ cups. Keeps about 2 weeks in the fridge.

  • 1 cupFresh cranberries
  • 1 cupSugar
  • 1 cupWater
  • 2–3 sprigsFresh rosemary

To garnish

  • Per glassA rosemary sprig, a few cranberries and a lime wheel

Method

  1. Make the syrup. Simmer the cranberries, sugar and water until the berries burst, about 10 minutes. Take it off the heat, add the rosemary and let it steep 15–20 minutes. Strain and chill. Don't boil the rosemary, or it turns bitter.
  2. Batch the base. In a pitcher, stir together the rum, lime juice, syrup, Cointreau and bitters. Chill at least an hour. It's best the same day, while the lime is fresh.
  3. Build the punch. Pour the base over a large block of ice or an ice ring in a punch bowl. Top with the ginger beer and give it one gentle stir, so it keeps its fizz.
  4. Serve. Ladle into ice-filled glasses. Clap a rosemary sprig between your hands to wake it up, then garnish with it, a few cranberries and a lime wheel. ¡Salud!

Notes

  • Taste before the guests arrive. Cranberries vary in tartness. If it's sharp, add syrup half an ounce at a time.
  • Make an ice ring. Freeze cranberries, lime wheels and rosemary in a Bundt pan the night before. It looks great and melts slower than cubes.
  • Smoke the rosemary. Hit each garnish sprig with a torch for a few seconds just before serving.
  • Zero-proof cups. Mix a little syrup, lime and ginger beer, with a splash of apple cider, for anyone not drinking.
