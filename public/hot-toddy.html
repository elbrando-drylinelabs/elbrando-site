el Brando Hot Toddy

100-proof whiskey, lemon and honey, a lemon coin buttoned up with cloves, and hot water to the top.

  • 3 min total
  • 1 mug
  • Built in the cup

Ingredients

  • 1 oz100-proof bottled-in-bond or straight whiskey
  • 1 ozLemon juice
  • ½ ozHoney
  • 1Lemon coin, with 6 cloves pressed in like buttons
  • To the topHot water

Method

  1. Button the coin. Press 6 cloves into the lemon coin like buttons.
  2. Build it in the cup. Add the whiskey, lemon juice, honey and the lemon coin. Everything goes in except the hot water.
  3. Top and enjoy. Fill to the top with hot water, and enjoy!
