Hot Toddy

Whiskey, honey, lemon and hot water. It takes five minutes and one mug, and it's the best thing to drink on a cold night.

  • 5 min total
  • 1 drink
  • No special gear

Ingredients

  • 1½ ozBourbon or whiskey (45 ml)
  • 1 tbspHoney
  • ½ ozFresh lemon juice (15 ml, about ¼ lemon)
  • 4–6 ozHot water, just off the boil (120–180 ml)
  • 1Lemon wheel
  • 3–4Whole cloves
  • 1Cinnamon stick (optional)

Method

  1. Warm the mug. Fill it with hot water, let it sit a minute, then pour it out.
  2. Melt the honey. Add the honey and a splash of hot water, then stir until it dissolves.
  3. Build the drink. Add the whiskey and lemon juice, then top up with 4–6 oz of hot water.
  4. Garnish and taste. Press the cloves into the lemon wheel, float it on top and add the cinnamon stick. Taste, then add a little more honey or lemon until it's right.

Notes

  • Whiskey: bourbon makes it rounder and sweeter, and rye makes it spicier. You can also swap in dark rum or brandy.
  • Water: water straight off a hard boil can taste flat and burn off some of the whiskey. Let the kettle rest for about 30 seconds first.
  • Tea version: use hot black tea or chamomile instead of water.
  • No alcohol: leave out the whiskey and add a little more honey, lemon and a slice of fresh ginger.
